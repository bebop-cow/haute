# Haute — a free sewing school

**Play it:** https://bebop-cow.github.io/haute/

A hands-on browser game that teaches you to sew real clothes — no classes, no sewing machine, no expensive fabric needed.

- **Six basics:** thread a needle and tie a knot, running stitch, backstitch, taking your measurements, sewing on a button, and putting in a zip.
- **Ten projects**, each drafted from *your* measurements:
  - Beginner: shift dress, boxy top, loose elastic-waist trousers
  - Beginner+: gathered sundress, A-line skirt (darts, side zip, waistband, button)
  - Intermediate: fitted dress, pencil skirt (zip and back vent), button-up shirt
  - Advanced: jeans, suit
- **Real-size patterns:** every project prints its full pattern tiled onto A4 or US Letter paper, with a test square, page map and alignment marks. You can also download one big SVG for a copy shop.
- After each step, a **"Doing it for real"** tip explains how to do that step on real fabric.

Everything can be sewn by hand. Old bedsheets make great practice fabric.

> The patterns are simplified drafts made by formula from your measurements. They haven't been tested like a commercial pattern, so sew a test version (a "toile") in cheap fabric first.

It's a single `index.html` file with no build step. It works on phones and desktops, and your progress is saved in your browser.
